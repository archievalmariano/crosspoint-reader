# v1.3.0: ON POINT collections, BULACAN routes and 2027 holidays

Changes since v1.2.0. This release also carries the v1.2.1 maintenance changes, which were never published on their
own.

## ON POINT

**Route collections.** ON POINT groups routes into BULACAN, NORTH LUZON and AIRPORT TRANSFERS.
- Choose one with the SELECT COLLECTION row.
- Each collection remembers its own main route.
- ON POINT opens straight to the active collection's main route.
- The choices are stored on the card in `/.crosspoint/onpoint.json`.

**BULACAN now has six P2P routes** (two list pages):

| Route | Operator | Leaving Bulacan | Leaving Metro Manila |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balagtas ↔ TriNoma | Bulacan P2P | PROVISIONAL | PROVISIONAL |
| Caypombo ↔ SM North EDSA | Precious Grace | PUBLISHED | PROVISIONAL |
| Pandi ↔ TriNoma *(new)* | Bulacan P2P | PROVISIONAL | PROVISIONAL |
| Plaridel ↔ TriNoma *(new)* | Bulacan P2P | PROVISIONAL | PROVISIONAL |
| Malolos ↔ TriNoma *(new)* | Precious Grace | PUBLISHED | PROVISIONAL |
| Guiguinto ↔ TriNoma *(new)* | Bulacan P2P | PROVISIONAL | PROVISIONAL |

**Other ON POINT changes:**
- Caypombo → SM North EDSA gains the **20:30** last trip, from Precious Grace's 2026 daily schedule.
- Balagtas ↔ TriNoma is now marked **PROVISIONAL**. Its departure times are unchanged.
- Departure lookups are faster.

**2027 holidays** (Proclamation No. 1427) are added to the Calendar and to ON POINT:
- Bulacan P2P routes run their Saturday/Sunday/holiday timetable on non-working holidays.
- Special working days, such as the EDSA anniversary on 25 February 2027, keep the weekday timetable.
- Precious Grace routes run their daily timetable every day.

## How to read the timetables

**PROVISIONAL: estimated departures; actual times may vary.**

**PUBLISHED:** the departure pattern follows the operator's published schedule.

## Sources and limitations

**Sources:**
- **Bulacan P2P:** first and last trips come from the operator's 30 August 2026 schedule graphics, by day type.
  - Departures in between follow a 30-minute pattern from published route listings. The operator's TriNoma terminal
    notice gives a **30–45-minute** interval, so some listed departures may not run.
  - **Weekend in-between departures** are an estimate.
- **Precious Grace:** first and last trips and the 30-minute interval come from the operator's 2026 daily schedule.
  - Its North EDSA departure board does not assign departures to particular destinations.
  - TriNoma is also a boarding point for the Caypombo route.

**Guiguinto ↔ TriNoma is an editorial reconstruction.**
- **Bulacan P2P publishes only its first and last trips:** 04:30–19:30 on weekdays and 05:00–19:30 on weekends and
  holidays from Guiguinto; 06:00 (06:30 on weekends and holidays) to 21:30 from TriNoma.
- **Weekday Guiguinto departures** follow the WalterMart Guiguinto terminal notice photographed in September 2025.
- **Weekend Guiguinto departures and all TriNoma departures** are reconstructed from those published first and last
  trips and the route's observed patterns.
- **None of the in-between Guiguinto times is confirmed by the operator.**

**Known discrepancy: TriNoma first trips.**
- An undated Bulacan P2P notice at TriNoma lists earlier first trips: Balagtas **05:30**, Plaridel **05:00**, and a
  partly obscured route, probably Pandi, **05:30**.
- The operator's dated 30 August 2026 graphics give **06:00**, and the board uses those.
- **Earlier buses may run from TriNoma before the first time shown,** and service frequency may differ.

**Dispatch can vary:** departures can depend on passenger loads and vehicle availability.

**The holiday calendar runs to 31 December 2027.** Eid'l Fitr and Eid'l Adha will be added when their dates are
proclaimed. 2028 holidays will follow in a later update.
