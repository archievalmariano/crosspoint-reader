# v1.6.0: three new ON POINT collections (X4 Pro only)

Changes since v1.5.0. This release is for the **XTEINK X4 Pro only**. It adds three ON POINT collections, METRO
MANILA, LAGUNA and SOUTH LUZON, with ten new route pairs. GOTO, DOGEAR and Tools work as they did in v1.5.0.

**XTEINK X4: no firmware in this release.** X4 devices stay on v1.4.0, and Check for Updates on an X4 does not offer
v1.6.0. The X4 firmware is in the
[v1.4.0 release](https://github.com/archievalmariano/crosspoint-reader/releases/tag/v1.4.0).

## New ON POINT collections

- **METRO MANILA:** ATC Alabang ↔ Greenbelt, VTX Alabang ↔ Starmall Shaw, and ATC Alabang ↔ SM Megamall.
- **LAGUNA:** Nuvali ↔ One Ayala, WM Cabuyao ↔ Makati, and WM Cabuyao ↔ Market Market.
- **SOUTH LUZON:** Batangas ↔ Newport, SM Lipa ↔ Newport, SM Masinag ↔ One Ayala, and Sierra Valley ↔ One Ayala.

ON POINT now has six collections and 25 route pairs (50 directions). The collection picker has two pages: BULACAN,
NORTH LUZON, AIRPORT TRANSFERS and METRO MANILA on the first, LAGUNA and SOUTH LUZON on the second.

Your collections and main routes stay as you set them. A new collection opens on its own main route until you choose
another.

## About these timetables

All 20 new directions are **PROVISIONAL**: the times come from operator lists, but no current operator statement
confirms them. Departures may vary. The 30 directions from earlier releases are unchanged: 18 PUBLISHED and 12
PROVISIONAL.

- **No holiday rules:** the RRCG routes (METRO MANILA and SOUTH LUZON) and both WM Cabuyao routes. On holidays the
  board shows the ordinary list for that day of the week, and the real service may differ.
- **Days without service:**
  - ATC Alabang ↔ SM Megamall has no Sunday service;
  - WM Cabuyao, SM Masinag and Sierra Valley run Monday to Friday only.
- **Nuvali ↔ One Ayala** shows no service on nationwide regular holidays and special non-working days in 2026 and
  2027, except Eid'l Fitr and Eid'l Adha, which this release does not include. The operator says "Holidays – no
  operations"; including special non-working days is our reading of that. On Metro Manila-only special days the
  board shows the ordinary list.
- **Sierra Valley ↔ One Ayala:** evening trips from One Ayala follow one list on Monday and Friday and another from
  Tuesday to Thursday.
- **Batangas ↔ Newport and SM Lipa ↔ Newport** use RRCG's posted daily boards. RRCG's website lists fewer trips at
  different times, so these stay PROVISIONAL.
- **Short labels** on the device: VTX ALABANG (RRCG's Starmall Alabang), WM CABUYAO (WalterMart Cabuyao), MAKATI (One
  Ayala / Landmark), MARKET MARKET (BGC Taguig), BATANGAS (Batangas Grand Terminal) and NEWPORT (Newport World Resorts,
  NAIA Terminal 3).
